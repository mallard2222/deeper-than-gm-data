Colts Have Fourth-Round Offer For Josh Downs; Keenan Allen Drawing Interest
  By 
Sam Robinson
 |
  at November 7, 2026 2:55 pm  
The 
Colts
have received at least one offer for
Jonathan Taylor
, and 
interest has come in on
Daniel Jones
. Indianapolis is open for business, but thus far, the team has only unloaded contract-year players. This pattern points 
Josh Downs
 out the door, especially as the walk-year wide receiver 
requested a trade
.
Still, the Colts are not planning to move him just to satisfy a request, but at least one offer has come in. More may be likely, especially considering the impediments some AFC wide receiver-needy teams have with the Jaguars’ 
Brian Thomas Jr.
 Thus far, the Colts have at least a fourth-round pick on the table.
An offer including a fourth has come in, 
per ESPN’s Adam Schefter and NFL.com’s Ian Rapoport
, and 
The Ringer’s Tom Pelissero indicates
 the Colts have received a proposal including a fourth and an additional draft asset. If the package includes a fourth and another pick, it would remind of the 2025 deals involving 
Rashid Shaheed
 (fourth-, fifth-rounders) and 
Jakobi Meyers
 (fourth, sixth).
Considering the Jaguars will probably avoid trading Thomas to an AFC threat, the 
Chiefs
 and Steelers come up as obvious candidates to have made an offer for Downs. Though, the Commanders and 49ers are looking for WR help too. The Rams, sitting at 4-4 and without 
Davante Adams
 for at least a few more weeks, figure to be at least looking around on this market as well.
The Chiefs 
came up as a Downs suitor this week
, and 
The Athletic’s Jonathan Jones adds
 the former third-round pick’s name is coming up most often regarding Kansas City’s latest wideout search. We heard earlier this week the Colts had been hoping for a third, and they will assuredly wait until the deadline nears to see if the market heats up to the point such an offer emerges. But having a Shaheed-like package would probably be a win for the Colts, considering Downs’ inconsistency and status as primarily a slot player.
Kansas City already has underneath targets in 
Travis Kelce
 and 
Rashee Rice
. They have not seen 
Xavier Worthy
, his 4.21-second Combine 40 time notwithstanding, become a viable deep threat. Downs would be an upgrade still for a Kansas City team that has been unable to find receiver consistency since trading 
Tyreek Hill
 (Chiefs-Hill reunion connections have slowed since the embattled free agent was found liable for civil battery in a suit brought upon by his ex-wife).
Downs is not the long-range threat Shaheed is, nor is his resume on Meyers’ level. But Downs posted 771 rookie-year yards with 
Gardner Minshew
 primarily targeting him and 806 in Year 2, being a key target for the historically inaccurate 
Anthony Richardson
. The Colts’ QB issues could work in their favor at the deadline, as Downs (25) could be marketed as a player with untapped potential. That will certainly be his representation’s pitch in 2027. And a move to a team with a better quarterback situation will clearly be desired by Downs’ camp ahead of that point.
As should be expected, Downs is not the only wide receiver the Colts are willing to move. 
Keenan Allen
 has come up as a player to watch regarding a trade, according to Schefter and Rapoport.
The 14th-year wideout is in an age-34 season and tied to a vet-minimum base salary. Allen would check in as a post-deadline buyout type in the NBA, and the Colts doing right by the former 
Shane Steichen
 Chargers pupil and cutting him after the deadline could be in play if no offer comes in. But Allen would be subject to waivers if he is cut after the deadline. It makes sense for the Colts to collect a late-round pick if they can by Tuesday.
Allen sought a return to Los Angeles in 2025, with the Rams being in play at the time. He ended up reuniting with the Chargers. The 49ers already have a host of 30-something playmakers, making Allen a bit redundant (though, San Francisco certainly needs healthy options at the position presently). A team that strikes out on Downs and Thomas could pivot to Allen or the Titans’ 
Calvin Ridley
, who 
came up as a backup plan this week
. With Thomas not certain to move and the Jets planning to retain 
Garrett Wilson
, Downs may be the top deadline target at the WR spot.
