The San Francisco 49ers will be without wide receivers Mike Evans (Achilles) and Deebo Samuel Sr. (hamstring) for Sunday's home game against the Las Vegas Raiders. General manager John Lynch said on KNBR that receivers Demarcus Robinson and KhaDarel Hodge will be available. The hope was both Evans and Samuel would be back after the 49ers' Week 8, but they will have to wait another week.

Malik Nabers (knee) returned to practice on Friday after missing Thursday. He told reporters that he'll "be good" to play Sunday against the Philadelphia Eagles. Running back Cam Skattebo (hamstring) was at practice, but did not participate and is listed as doubtful. Devin Singletary and Greg Derosiers Jr. would serve as the top two running backs if the second-year Arizona State back cannot go.

Other Week 9 injuries to monitor:

  • Cleveland Browns quarterback Deshaun Watson (concussion) has cleared protocol and is questionable for Sunday. Shedeur Sanders will start for the first time since Week 18. Watson or Dillion Gabriel will serve as the backup.

  • While the New York Jets will get Breece Hall (quad) back after a five-game absence, wide receiver Garrett Wilson (concussion) is out for the team's road game against the Kansas City Chiefs. Wilson entered protocol after taking a hit to the head during last week's loss to the Las Vegas Raiders. He reported experiencing symptoms when he reported to the team's practice facility on Monday.

  • Green Bay Packers linebacker Micah Parsons will not play this week, but did return to practice for the first time since tearing his ACL and meniscus in his left knee last season. He is eyeing a Thanksgiving Eve game comeback against the Los Angeles Rams on Nov. 25.

  • Emeka Egbuka (foot) has a chance to take the field in Chicago against the Bears in Week 9. The Tampa Bay Buccaneers wide receiver was a full participant in practice on Thursday and Friday after missing the past two weeks with a concussion and a foot injury. He is listed as questionable.
