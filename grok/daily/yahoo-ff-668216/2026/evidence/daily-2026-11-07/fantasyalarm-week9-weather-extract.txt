Week 9 is a quiet weather week. Only Thursday night's Jaguars-Ravens game has a rain chance above 30 percent at kickoff; wind reaches 10 mph with gusts of 15 to 20 mph in just three games, and four games are indoors or have a roof. The biggest line moves this week come from quarterback injuries, not the forecast. The Jaguars-Ravens total is down 9 points after 
Lamar Jackson
's thumb injury, and Cardinals-Seahawks is down 5 with both starting quarterbacks changed. Here is every game and what it means for your DFS builds.

Worst Weather Games in Week 9
Nothing on this slate is a serious weather threat, but a few games are worth a look.
Thursday Night: Jaguars at Ravens
Jaguars at Ravens has the highest rain chance on the board, with rain at 17 percent in the first hour and 35 to 40 percent from 9 PM on, so any rain is more likely to show up in the second half. Wind is light. The bigger story is Baltimore's quarterback room. 
Lamar Jackson

Tyler Huntley

Joe Fagnano

Tyler Loop

Trevor Lawrence

Bhayshul Tuten

Chris Rodriguez

Rain and Snow Impact on Fantasy Football
There is no snow this week, and rain is a minor factor. Jaguars-Ravens has the only meaningful chance, mostly in the second half, and the Cardinals-Seahawks chance is lower and fades late; light, intermittent showers rarely change play-calling. If steady rain does show up on Thursday, it would favor Jacksonville's run game and further limit a Baltimore passing game that is already short on quarterback talent. There is no need to adjust kickers or receivers for rain elsewhere.
Wind Impact on Fantasy Football
Wind is a small story, and it is mostly an early-game factor. Giants-Eagles and Rams-Commanders have gusts of 20 mph around kickoff that ease to 15 mph by 4 PM; Packers-Patriots have gusts of 18 mph early, and Cardinals-Seahawks have gusts of 17 mph early that fade to 7 mph by 7 PM. That is enough to nudge deep throws and longer field goals in the first half but not enough to change how teams play. Every other outdoor game has sustained wind of 8 mph or less, so wind should not factor into lineup decisions.
NFL Games to Avoid in DFS Because of Weather
No game is a true weather fade this week. The closest calls are below, and most of the concern is about quarterbacks, not the forecast.
Giants at Eagles: gusts to 20 mph and the lowest total on the board, with two offenses led by 
J.J. McCarthy
 and 
Joe Milton

Cardinals at Seahawks: a 23 percent rain chance and gusts of 15 to 17 mph with 
Drew Lock
 and 
Gardner Minshew
 starting.
Jaguars at Ravens: modest rain, but a Baltimore offense led by 
Joe Fagnano
 is a tough environment for any Ravens skill player.
Lighter downgrade: Rams at Commanders, where gusts of 20 mph early and Stafford's arm are worth a look.
Everything else, including the four indoor or roofed games and the dry, calm forecasts across the rest of the slate, should play out without weather being a factor in how you build your lineups.
