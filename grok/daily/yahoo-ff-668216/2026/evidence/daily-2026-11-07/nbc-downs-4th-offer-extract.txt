                ESPN’s Adam Schefter reports the Colts have told teams they’ve received an offer of a fourth-round pick for WR Josh Downs. 
                The Colts’ original asking price for Downs, who has requested a trade, was a third-round pick. Indianapolis has told teams they already have an offer of a fourth-round pick for the 25-year-old receiver, who is in the last year of his rookie contract. Downs is probably one of the more likelier players to be traded ahead of the deadline. Teams have also called the Colts about Keenan Allen, though he’s unlikely to bring back a similar trade return given his age.

Sports Illustrated’s Albert Breer reports that Josh Downs “leads the wide receiver market” ahead of the NFL trade deadline.
Downs’ 2026 season has fallen short of expectations, but perhaps he has a chance to rebound. Breer believes acquiring Downs would “probably” cost a fourth-round pick. He is “one of a number of receivers on the market” at the moment, with the Chiefs and Steelers among the teams looking for help at the position. Breer adds that the 49ers are “open” to adding a wide receiver as well, but with their positional health improving, San Francisco is “less desperate than they were a couple of weeks ago.” The Steelers’ wide receiver corps might be too muddled for Downs to meaningfully increase his productivity, but landing in Kansas City or San Francisco would likely be good for him.

The Athletic’s Nate Atkins believes the Rams must find a way to trade for a wide receiver before the November 10th deadline.
The Rams’ resources are thin. They already traded away their 2027 first- and second-round picks, their 2028 second-round pick and their 2029 third-round pick. They also have just $8,000 in 2026 salary cap space, so restructuring the potential new add’s deal would be required. Atkins believes the Rams should consider Josh Downs as the top option due to his ability to beat man coverage and contribute as a returner. Other options, according to Atkins, include Calvin Ridley, Keenan Allen and Jauan Jennings, “for the likely cost of a Day 3 pick.”

A to Z Sports’ Charles Goldman reports that if the Chiefs trade for a wide receiver, the team “would like to acquire a long-term fixture rather than a one-year rental.”
Chiefs coach Andy Reid told reporters today that general manager Brett Veach has been constantly sharing potential trade targets with the coaching staff for about a month. According to Goldman, the Chiefs could also target additions along the offensive and defensive lines, but he believes the team would “likely have to acquire a receiver through other means” before focusing on the trenches. There aren’t many free agency options right now, though, so it is unclear how this scenario would play out. Goldman notes that Josh Downs and Brian Thomas Jr. “have surfaced in trade discussions.” He does not think the Chiefs will make a move before Sunday, though a move early next week, before the November 10th trade deadline, is still possible.

Josh Downs caught 4-of-5 targets for 23 yards and a touchdown in the Colts’ Week 8 loss to the Jaguars.
