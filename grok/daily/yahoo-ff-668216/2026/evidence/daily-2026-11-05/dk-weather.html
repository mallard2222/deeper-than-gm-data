Jacksonville Jaguars vs. Baltimore Ravens Weather Forecast

Weather Impact: Medium

The Jacksonville Jaguars vs. Baltimore Ravens Thursday Night Football matchup at M&T Bank Stadium comes with an unusually warm November night in Baltimore and a scattered storm threat. Kickoff is forecast at 68°F with a light S wind at 5 mph and a 17% precipitation chance, but showers and thunderstorm chances climb back to 35-40% after 9 PM ET. Wind is a non-factor, so the rain risk alone drives the medium-impact rating.

Hourly Weather Forecast

Weather Timeline

  • 6-7 PM ET — Warmups run at 70°F to 69°F with a S wind easing from 8 mph to 5 mph, while rain chances peak early at 33-38% with thunderstorms possible at 7 PM.
  • 8:15 PM ET (Kickoff) — 68°F with a S wind at 5 mph and the lowest rain chance of the night at 17%.
  • 9-10 PM ET — Showers and thunderstorm chances return to 35% as the wind fades to 2-3 mph and the temperature holds near 67-68°F.
  • 11 PM ET — 66°F with a near-calm S wind and the night’s highest rain chance at 40%, so any storm that develops is most likely late.

DFS and Betting Weather Impact

Wind will not touch the passing game or field goal range, so the only variable is a possible fourth-quarter shower or storm that could slick the ball and footing late. That is a minor nudge toward the under at most, and it matters more for a Ravens offense starting undrafted rookie Joe Fagnano at quarterback with Jake Moody filling in at kicker than for the matchup overall. Any lightning would also bring a delay risk worth tracking for live bettors.

Jacksonville Jaguars vs. Baltimore Ravens Weather FAQs

Does M&T Bank Stadium have a roof?

No. M&T Bank Stadium is an open-air stadium, so rain, wind and temperature all reach the natural grass playing surface.

What weather conditions could impact Jacksonville Jaguars vs. Baltimore Ravens?

Rain and thunderstorms are the variables to watch Thursday. The forecast calls for a 17% precipitation chance at kickoff rising to 35-40% after 9 PM ET, with light S wind of 2-5 mph and temperatures between 66°F and 68°F.
