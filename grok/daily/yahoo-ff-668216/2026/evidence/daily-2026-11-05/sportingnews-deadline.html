NFL trade rumors tracker: Live updates on news for Jonathan Taylor, Jacoby Brissett and more before 2026 deadline

Dan Treacy

•
Add Sporting News on Google

JUMP TO:

  • Every completed trade
  • Latest news and rumors

NFL trade season started early, as Jeffery Simmons, Marvin Harrison Jr., Quenton Nelson and DeForest Buckner all found themselves on the move well in advance of the Nov. 10 deadline.

As the final buzzer approaches, though, contenders are looking to best position themselves to make a run.

The top name on the market might be Colts RB Jonathan Taylor, who told the team he doesn't want to be traded but might not have much of a choice as Indianapolis' season spirals. Other teams, including the Cowboys and Eagles, have interesting decisions to make on their path forward as they weigh whether to trade stars.

The Sporting News is tracking live updates on the latest NFL trade rumors ahead of the 2026 deadline. Follow along below with the news.

MORE: SN's midseason All-Pro team

NFL trades completed before 2026 deadline

— Nov. 4: The Arizona Cardinals traded QB Jacoby Brissett to the Baltimore Ravens.

— Oct. 29: The Tennessee Titans traded DT Jeffery Simmons and a 2028 fifth-round pick to the Chicago Bears for two first-round picks and a 2027 fourth-round pick.

— Oct. 27: The Indianapolis Colts traded DT DeForest Buckner to the San Francisco 49ers for a fourth-round pick.

— Oct. 27: The Indianapolis Colts traded OG Quenton Nelson to the Seattle Seahawks for third- and fifth-round picks.

— Oct. 11: The Arizona Cardinals traded WR Marvin Harrison Jr. to the Houston Texans for second- and sixth-round picks.

— Oct. 7: The Philadelphia Eagles traded OG Cam Jurgens and a seventh-round pick to the Baltimore Ravens for a 2028 second-round pick and 2027 fifth-round pick.

— Sept. 30: The Pittsburgh Steelers traded CB Joey Porter Jr. to the Dallas Cowboys for a 2028 second-round pick and 2027 sixth-round pick.

— Sept. 28: The Minnesota Vikings traded QB J.J. McCarthy to the New York Giants for a 2027 fifth-round pick.

Latest NFL trade rumors before 2026 deadline

Conflicting reports over Giants' willingness to play ball with Colts for Jonathan Taylor

Nov. 5 — Taylor remains the name on the lips of decision-makers ahead of Tuesday's trade deadline, with SNY's Connor Hughes reporting that sources not connected to the Giants are unsure of whether New York will attempt to make a move for the Colts standout. Some believe head coach John Harbaugh wants to move aggressively to land the former Offensive Player of the Year winner. Others believe Indianapolis' asking price will prove too cost-prohibitive for the Giants — or any other side — to seek out his services.

Colts to wait until after Week 9 before exploring trades for Jonathan Taylor, others

Nov. 5 — The Colts have been monitoring interest in a series of players, headlined by Jonathan Taylor and Josh Downs. However, according to insider Jordan Schultz, Indianapolis doesn't plan on making a move until after its Week 9 game vs. the Cowboys. It appears that if teams want to strike a deal with the Colts, they'll have to work quickly on Monday and Tuesday.

Ravens acquiring Jacoby Brissett from Cardinals

Nov. 4 — The Ravens are finalizing a deal to acquire QB Jacoby Brissett from the Cardinals, ESPN's Adam Schefter reports, though it won't be completed in time for Brissett to join the team before Thursday's game. Compensation has not yet been reported.

Colts' asking price for Taylor remains high

Nov. 4 — The Colts are still asking for a first-round pick for Jonathan Taylor and maintaining that they don't have to trade him, ESPN's Jeremy Fowler reports, while suitors believe a second-round pick plus another asset should be enough.

Teams 'have their eye' on Jets

Nov. 4 — ESPN's Dan Graziano reports teams were watching the Jets last weekend, wondering whether another loss might open the door for players such as Will McDonald IV to become available. With Braelon Allen's breakout in Breece Hall's absence, Hall could be another trade candidate.

Cardinals' Josh Sweat open to trade

Nov. 3 — Cardinals pass-rusher Josh Sweat is open to the possibility of being traded, according to Jordan Schultz. Sweat, whose name surfaced in rumors leading up to the season, had 12 sacks in 2025 but has seen his production dip this year.

Teams intrigued by George Pickens' situation

Nov. 2 — People around the NFL are wondering whether the Cowboys might make George Pickens available, Jordan Schultz reports. While there is no firm indication Dallas is looking to trade Pickens or anyone, the team's lack of success and Pickens' expiring contract could put a deal on the table.

MORE: Why the Cowboys should - and shouldn't - trade George Pickens

Several teams in on Jonathan Taylor

Nov. 1 — Jordan Schultz reports the Broncos, Vikings, Packers, Bengals and Giants are among teams who have reached out to the Colts about Jonathan Taylor. Schultz believes a trade is "more likely than not" despite Indianapolis' asking price being too high for most teams at the moment.
