    Kenneth Gainwell (hip) was limited in Wednesday’s practice.

  • Kenny Gainwell
    TB Running Back #1
    Buccaneers head coach Todd Bowles said RB Kenneth Gainwell’s usage is getting closer to the “1A, 1B” usage the team envisioned when signing him.
    Gainwell saw 25 carries in a run-heavy game against the Falcons in Week 8. Bowles said, “I think we finally got to where we’re utilizing Kenny more and more, and that’s kind of the 1A, 1B we were talking about when we first signed him.” Gainwell saw 50-percent of the snaps for the first time all season last week and now has back-to-back weeks of at least 14 carries. He is still seeing low utilization in the passing game and game script has certainly played a role in his recent role, but it is notable that Bowles mentions the Bucs envisioned a 1A/1B backfield pairing between Bucky Irving and Gainwell. Despite Bowles’ comments, fantasy managers should anticipate Irving operating with the majority of backfield touches, like he did from Weeks 1-6. We will still have to see Gainwell’s usage in a more neutral game script to feel confident about him as a fantasy option moving forward.
  • Kenny Gainwell
    TB Running Back #1
    Kenneth Gainwell rushed 25 times for 140 yards in the Bucs’ Week 8 win over the Falcons.
    Bucky Irving gave the Bucs a 16-0 lead entering the fourth quarter and passed the baton to Gainwell, who racked up 18 carries in the final frame. Irving only touched the ball once over the final 15 minutes. Gainwell’s late-game dominance got him up to career-highs in carries and yards. He also managed a season-high 14 attempts under similar circumstances last week. Unfortunately for Gainwell, the Bucs aren’t likely to be playing with a big lead in Week 9 against the Bears. Gainwell will return to the RB3/4 border next week.
  • Kenny Gainwell
    TB Running Back #1
    Kenneth Gainwell (hip) is active for Week 8 against the Falcons.
    Right tackle Luke Goedeke is also active. Gainwell will continue mixing in behind Bucky Irving this week. He has yet to hit a dozen fantasy points in a game this year and there’s no reason to expect that to change anytime soon. Gainwell is a low-end RB4 for Week 8. Inactive for the Bucs are DL DeMonte Capehart, WR Emeka Egbuka, CB Ayden Garnes, OT Luke Haggard, and OG Billy Schrauth.
  • Kenny Gainwell
    TB Running Back #1
    ESPN’s Jeremy Fowler reports Kenneth Gainwell (hip) is “good to go” for Week 8 against the Falcons.
    Week 7 was Gainwell’s first game with more than seven touches all year. He handled 14 opportunities, all of which were carries, and they came in a 30-point blowout win over the Panthers. Unless the Bucs stay hot and roll over the Falcons, expect Gainwell’s touch total to return to the single digits. He is barely in the RB4 mix for Week 8.

  • Kenny Gainwell
    TB Running Back #1

Gainwell racked up 14 carries in a blowout win over the Panthers last week, but that hasn’t been the norm for him.   • Kenny Gainwell
    TB Running Back #1

    Gainwell suffered the hip injury while logging a season-high 14 rush attempts in Week 7. The Bucs blew out the Panthers and racked up 49 carries as a team. Bucky Irving would be in line for more work on passing downs if Gainwell is out for Week 8. Sean Tucker could also see an uptick in snaps, though his normal role has very little overlap with what Gainwell does. Per usual, Irving looks like a high-end RB2 this week.
  • Kenny Gainwell
    TB Running Back #1
    Kenneth Gainwell rushed 14 times for 88 yards in the Bucs’ Week 7 win over the Panthers.
    Gainwell was not targeted in the passing game, but that’s because Baker Mayfield attempted only 11 throws. That in turn was due to the fact that Tampa road paved Carolina on the ground as the Panthers committed four turnovers. It’s why Gainwell rushed for a season-high 88 yards after never perviously reaching 20. He was still out-carried 24-14 by Bucky Irving, who found the end zone twice as Sean Tucker hit pay dirt once. We are sure this is a formula the Bucs would like to replicate moving forward, but it is not going to be doable most of the time. It does at least make Gainwell a slightly more realistic FLEX option heading into Week 8 against the Falcons.
  • Kenny Gainwell

He hasn’t caught more than three passes in a game either, so even the PPR scam that he operated in Pittsburgh has been shut down. Gainwell isn’t in the fantasy conversation headed into Week 7 versus Carolina.
