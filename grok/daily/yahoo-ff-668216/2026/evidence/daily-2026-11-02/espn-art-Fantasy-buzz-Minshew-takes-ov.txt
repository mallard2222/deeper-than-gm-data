FlorioThe Arizona Cardinals named Gardner Minshew II as their starting quarterback on Monday for the Week 9 game in Seattle against the Seahawks.Minshew took over for Jacoby Brissett on Sunday against Dallas and threw for 188 yards and three touchdowns in a comeback win against the Cowboys, but faces a much tougher task next week against Seattle.The biggest question is what this means for the Cardinals' pass catchers, but here's how I expect the switch at QB to impact all positions on offense.While a QB change always adds some volatility, Trey McBride remains a must-start tight end. McBride is a must start regardless of who the QB is, but Minshew's tendency to target tight ends at a high rate keeps him as an elite option. In both 2023 and 2024, Minshew threw to tight ends 12% of the time, which is actually a higher rate than Brissett this season (10%).Due to volume, Michael Wilson will remain in play in most matchups. However, next week against Seattle is a week to get him out of your starting lineup. The Cardinals next three weeks are tough matchups for Wilson. Outside of Wilson, fantasy managers should avoid the rest of the Cardinals receivers for the time being.The running backs could see a bump in targets as well, as Minshew has always targeted running backs at a high rate. His career average is 17%, while Brissett is at 13% this season.As for Minshew himself, fantasy managers should stay away with the next three weeks being the Seahawks, Rams and Chiefs. For Week 9, some more favorable streaming options are Kyler Murray (or potentially Carson Wentz), Malik Willis, Sam Darnold and C.J. Stroud.It is worth noting that since 2021, there have been 12 relief performances of 15 or more fantasy points by quarterbacks who went on to make another start at some point in that given season; that group averaged 6.7 fewer fantasy points as starters (shout out to Tristan H. Cockcroft for the stat!).Nov. 2: Seahawks return running backs Charbonnet, Price on Monday Night FootballRookie running back Jadarian Price will return from the IR on Monday Night Football. With Zach Charbonnet making his debut, Seattle will have their full backfield for the first time this season. Due to that, there is uncertainty with how the workload will be distributed. Both Price and Charbonnet have been seldomly used in the passing game, so they could be fighting for carries.Unless the Seahawks opt to ease Charbonnet in, I would expect the goal line looks to go his way. That makes him the preferred fantasy play. However, if Price is in more of a secondary role, he could see passing downs. Charbonnet is the safer floor play, while Price is a bit more boom-or-bust.
