 Colts WR Keenan Allen ruled out of London game because of groin injury Updated Oct. 3, 2026 11:52 a.m. ET share facebook x reddit link Associated Press LONDON (AP) — Indianapolis Colts wide receiver Keenan Allen has been ruled out of Sunday’s game against the Washington Commanders at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium because of a groin injury. The Colts said Saturday the six-time Pro Bowler was downgraded after being limited in practice this week and then sitting out Friday, when he was listed as questionable for the game. Allen caught six passes for 63 yards and a touchdown in last week’s 19-17 win over the Houston Texans . Indy then elevated wide receiver Nick Westbrook-Ikhine to the active roster from the practice squad for the game. He also was active last week. Ashton Dulin's status also was listed as questionable with an ankle issue, though the receiver was a full participant at practice Thursday and Friday.
