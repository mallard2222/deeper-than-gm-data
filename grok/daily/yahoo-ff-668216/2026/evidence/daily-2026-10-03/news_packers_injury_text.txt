Evan Siegle, packers.com LB Edgerrin Cooper Oct. 3 update: LB Edgerrin Cooper  (shoulder/concussion) has cleared concussion protocol and has been removed from the injury report. GREEN BAY – The Packers have ruled out guards  Aaron Banks (knee/toe) and Jacob Monk (quadricep), and defensive linemen Warren Brinson (calf/groin) and Anthony Campbell  (ankle) for Sunday's matchup with the Tampa Bay Buccaneers. Linebacker Edgerrin Cooper (shoulder/concussion) and center/guard  Jager Burton (groin) are questionable, though Cooper promoted to full participation in Friday's practice. Banks missed the regular-season opener in Minnesota with a knee injury before injuring his toe 22 snaps into Green Bay's Week 2 matchup with the New York Jets. He hasn't practiced since. Monk didn't practice this week after leaving in the fourth quarter of the Packers' Week 3 game against Atlanta. Second-year veteran Donovan Jennings  finished the game at right guard. With injuries piling up on the offensive line, the Packers signed veteran guards Kevin Zeitler and Laken Tomlinson to their 53-man roster and added former first-round pick Mekhi Becton Sr. to their practice squad. All three practiced in full this week. "We have a plan in place right now, and if everything goes the way we plan it to go, then we'll be set," said Head Coach Matt LaFleur on Friday morning. "Got one more practice, let's get through that and then finalize our decisions." LaFleur also said Brinson will miss a "significant amount of time" after suffering a groin injury during practice Wednesday. The 2025 sixth-round pick has yet to play this season after initially injuring his calf during warmups prior to an Aug. 21 preseason game in Denver. "All he did was slip and injuries occur, and a lot of times it's non-contact," LaFleur said. "That was one of our deeper rooms thankfully, you guys could all see it throughout the offseason and into the preseason. You just have less bodies in the room, and it puts a little bit more of a load on everybody else." Cooper was limited in practice this week while working his way through the concussion protocol. Burton was added to the injury report on Thursday. Edge rusher Lukas Van Ness (shoulder) and defensive tackle Karl Brooks (illness) didn't receive a game status and will play Sunday. The Buccaneers ruled out quarterback Baker Mayfield (right thumb), linebacker Rueben Bain (groin/shoulder), cornerback Benjamin Morrison (quad) and tight end Ko Kieft (elbow). Linebacker SirVocea Dennis (calf/personal), defensive tackle Rakeem Nunez-Roches (ankle/foot), and cornerback Jacob Parrish (personal) are questionable. Tampa Bay didn't issue a game status for the following players who will be available: receiver Chris Godwin Jr. (ankle), running back Bucky Irving (glute), defensive tackle DeMonte Capehart (thumb), linebacker Josiah Trotter (shoulder), linebacker Yaya Diaby (calf/shoulder), cornerback Keionte Scott (toe) and defensive tackle A'Shawn Robinson (rest).
