ORCHARD PARK, N.Y. -- Baltimore Ravens quarterback Lamar Jackson needs to have surgery on his injured right thumb and will be placed on injured reserve, coach Jesse Minter said after his team's 36-10 loss to the Buffalo Bills . 
Jackson hurt his thumb on his first play in Sunday's game. While throwing a pass, Jackson had his right hand struck by the hand of Bills outside linebacker Greg Rousseau , who was attempting to knock down the throw. 
The two-time NFL MVP immediately looked down at his hand and went to the sideline. He went into the tunnel with his right hand wrapped and held by a trainer. 
It only got worse for the Ravens in the second quarter, when Tyler "Snoop" Huntley , who was 2-for-4 for zero yards, left the game with a right toe injury. Huntley was ruled out in the third quarter. 
With Jackson and Huntley injured, Baltimore turned to No. 3 quarterback Joe Fagnano , an undrafted rookie out of Connecticut . Fagnano, who made his first regular-season appearance after an impressive preseason, finished the game 10 of 21 passing for 91 yards and an interception. 
This was Jackson's first game back after missing the past three games with a high ankle sprain. It's a devastating loss for Baltimore, which had scored only five touchdowns in the previous 14 quarters without Jackson. 
Jackson returned to the sideline in the third quarter with what appeared to be a brace on his right hand. 
It's been nine years since a team has had this run of injuries at quarterback this early in a game. The last time a team had three quarterbacks throw a pass in a first half was the 2017 Dallas Cowboys . 
The Ravens (4-3) are looking to avoid a three-game losing streak.
