49ers Injury Report

Christian McCaffrey, Nick Bosa to play in 49ers vs. Raiders, but Mike Evans out

The San Francisco 49ers won't have three veteran wide receivers Sunday, but they will have Christian McCaffrey and Nick Bosa available.

By Matt Maiocco, 49ers Insider • Published 29 minutes ago • Updated 29 minutes ago

Share

SANTA CLARA — Three of the 49ers’ six wide receivers on their 53-man roster won't be available to face the Las Vegas Raiders on Sunday.

The 49ers on Friday ruled out receivers Mike Evans (Achilles), Deebo Samuel (hamstring) and Christian Kirk (knee) for the team’s Week 9 game at Levi’s Stadium.

The 49ers appear likely to activate veteran receiver Demarcus Robinson from the injured reserve list after he missed the past five games with an ankle injury. San Francisco must create a roster spot for Robinson by 1 p.m. PT on Saturday.

San Francisco could also elevate veteran receiver KhaDarel Hodge from the practice squad. Hodge serves as one of the team’s top special-teams players.

Meanwhile, coach Kyle Shanahan said running back Christian McCaffrey and defensive end Nick Bosa were cleared to play Sunday.

McCaffrey (hamstring) and Bosa (calf) were limited at practice throughout the week.

McCaffrey sustained a mild hamstring strain in the 49ers’ Week 7 game.
