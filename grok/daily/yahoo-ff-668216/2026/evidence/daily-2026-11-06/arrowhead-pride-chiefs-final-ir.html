Chiefs-Jets Week 9 Final Injury Report: Mansoor Delane ruled out

The final injury news for the Week 9 game between Kansas City and New York.

by Ron Kopp Jr.
Nov 6, 2026, 7:56 PM UTC

Each practice day this season, the Kansas City Chiefs release an official injury report ahead of the next game. In Week 9, the Chiefs play the New York Jets on Sunday afternoon.

Here is the Chiefs’ first official injury report of the week, courtesy of the team’s official website:

Chiefs

  • Rookie defensive tackle Peter Woods (neck) was ruled out after missing practice all week. He suffered the injury early in the Week 8 loss; he walked gingerly to the sideline without stopping the game after taking on an open-field block.
  • Rookie cornerback Mansoor Delane (shoulder) was ruled out after limited participation in practice all week. He has been dealing on and off with the same shoulder injury since training camp and missed last week’s game against the Denver Broncos.

“Both those two… are making progress, and that’s a positive thing, but they’re going to be out this game,” Reid said during his Friday press conference.

“It’s just a matter of getting back and making sure they’re strong; both of them, it’s a strength thing right now,” Reid said before alluding to the fact that both could be fine over the next week or so.

  • Defensive tackle Omarr Norman-Lott (knee) was ruled out after limited practice for the two weeks since being designated to return from the Reserve/Injured list; next week is the final week before he must be activated on the 53-man roster.
  • Returner Nikko Remigio (hip) was listed as questionable after upgrading to full participation in practice Friday and Thursday from being limited Wednesday. The team signed undrafted rookie wideout Jacob De Jesus to the practice squad this week, who has preseason reps as a returner for the Chiefs.

  • Wide receiver Rashee Rice (hamstring/knee) was a full participant in practice on Friday and Thursday after being marked as limited on Wednesday. He has no game designation heading into Week 9.
  • Tight end Travis Kelce (ankle) was a full participant in practice on Friday and Thursday after being marked as limited on Wednesday. He has no game designation heading into Week 9.

The following Chiefs players were full participants in practice all week and have no game designation:

  • Linebacker Nick Bolton (neck)
  • Linebacker Drue Tranquill (pectoral)
  • Quarterback Patrick Mahomes (knee)

Jets

  • No. 1 wide receiver Garrett Wilson entered concussion protocol on Monday after a helmet-to-helmet hit in Week 8 and was ruled out for this week. He has racked up 527 yards and three touchdowns over 43 receptions this season.
  • Starting defensive tackle Harrison Phillips (ankle) was listed as questionable after he did not participate in Wednesday or Thursday practice, but was a full participant on Friday. He has 24 tackles, three quarterback hits, and a fumble recovered while playing 57% of the defensive snaps for New York this season. The Jets placed defensive tackle T’Vondre Sweat (knee) on the Reserve/Injured list last week.
  • Rookie phenom edge rusher David Bailey (groin) has no game designation after he was upgraded to a full participant in Thursday’s practice after being limited Wednesday and missing Week 8. He has 5.5 sacks and seven tackles for loss while playing 81% of the Jets’ defensive snaps.
  • Tight end Mason Taylor (back) has no game designation and was a full participant in Thursday’s and Friday’s practice after being limited on Wednesday; he missed time earlier in the season due to a thumb injury.
  • Starting running back Breece Hall (knee) has been on the IR since Week 3 but will return this week after fully particpating in practice on Thursday and Friday.He has 265 total yards and a touchdown this season. His backup, Braelon Allen, has 565 rushing yards and three touchdowns in relief.
  • Rookie safety VJ Payne of Kansas State saw his practice window open for the first time this season after suffering an injury in the preseason; he was listed as questionable to play.
