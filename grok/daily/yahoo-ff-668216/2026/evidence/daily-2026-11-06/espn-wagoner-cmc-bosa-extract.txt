HEADLINE: Nick Bosa back, Christian McCaffrey good to go for Niners
DESC: The 49ers will have a healthy Nick Bosa for the first time since Week 2, and running back Christian McCaffrey is good to go this week against the Raiders.

KEY QUOTES/FACTS from story body:
- McCaffrey (hamstring) does not have an injury designation after minor hamstring strain Week 7 vs ATL.
- Did not practice Wednesday; participated Thursday and Friday; cleared.
- McCaffrey: "It wasn't too bad... having the bye week was awesome, but I feel great."
- Bosa likewise no designation; OUT since before Week 3; "I feel great."
- OUT: Mike Evans (Achilles), Deebo Samuel (hamstring), Christian Kirk (knee/meniscus Wed practice; surgery unclear).
- Likely activate Demarcus Robinson (ankle) from IR.
