 The 2026 NFL trade deadline is Nov. 10 at 4 p.m. ET. We already have eight deals -- and more are on the way. The Giants traded for Vikings quarterback J.J. McCarthy , the Steelers traded Joey Porter Jr . to the Cowboys, the Eagles sent center Cam Jurgens to the Ravens and the Texans acquired wide receiver Marvin Harrison Jr . The Colts traded away guard Quenton Nelson and defensive tackle DeForest Buckner , too. The biggest deal yet happened on Oct. 29, with the Titans sending defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons to the Bears and landing two first-round picks in return. And in the most recent trade, the Ravens got some much-needed QB help by adding Cardinals quarterback Jacoby Brissett . We're tracking all in-season moves and trade-related buzz right through the deadline. The most recent deals will be added at the top. More coverage: Transactions | Depth charts Grading every big trade Nov. 4 Cardinals trade QB Brissett to Ravens Ravens get: QB Jacoby Brissett Cardinals get: TBD The Cardinals are finalizing a trade to send QB Jacoby Brissett to the Ravens, sources told ESPN's Adam Schefter and NFL Network's Ian Rapoport. The deal is expected to be completed this weekend. Brissett's first start as the Ravens' starting quarterback could come as soon as Week 10 when Baltimore hosts the Chargers on "Monday Night Football." He was recently benched by the Cardinals before the trade. Oct. 29 Titans trade DT Simmons to Bears Bears get: DT Jeffery Simmons , 2028 fifth-round pick Titans get: 2027 first-round pick, 2027 fourth-round pick, 2028 first-round pick The Bears are acquiring All-Pro defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons from the Titans, sources told ESPN's Adam Schefter. The Titans are receiving 2027 and 2028 first-round picks, plus a 2027 fourth-round pick from the Bears in exchange for Simmons and a 2028 fifth-round pick, the sources told Schefter. Simmons, 29, signed a three-year, $105.8 million extension with $100 million guaranteed with the Titans in June. He is under contract through the 2030 season. Oct. 7 Eagles trade C Jurgens to Ravens Eagles get: 2027 fifth-round pick, 2028 second-round pick Ravens get: C Cam Jurgens , 2027 seventh-round pick The Eagles are trading two-time Pro Bowl center Jurgens and a 2027 seventh-round pick to the Ravens in exchange for a 2027 fifth-round pick and a 2028 second-round pick, sources told ESPN's Adam Schefter on Wednesday. The Eagles had moved Jurgens to left guard after Landon Dickerson was placed on injured reserve because of a knee injury. Jurgens self-reported concussion symptoms Monday morning, was evaluated and is now in the concussion protocol, a source told NFL Network's Mike Garafolo on Monday. The Steelers are trading Porter to the Cowboys for a 2027 sixth-round pick and a 2028 second-round selection, sources confirmed to NFL Network's Cameron Wolfe and ESPN's Todd Archer. The trade marks the end of a monthslong saga that stretched throughout the Steelers' offseason as Porter, one of the team's 2023 second-round picks, awaited an extension and was sidelined with a back injury.
