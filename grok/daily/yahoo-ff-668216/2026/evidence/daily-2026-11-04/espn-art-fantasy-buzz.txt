 Everything that happens in the NFL has additional context when viewed from a fantasy football perspective. From position battles to injuries and so much more, the news cycle will constantly affect player values. Our fantasy football buzz file, with contributions from our ESPN fantasy writers and our NFL Nation reporters, aims to provide fantasy managers with the intel they need as news breaks around the league. Nov. 4: Garrett Wilson 's concussion could create more opportunities for Jets playmakers By Eric Moody New York Jets head coach Aaron Glenn announced Wednesday that Wilson is in the concussion protocol. Wilson played the entire game against the Las Vegas Raiders last week, finishing with six catches for 83 yards on 12 targets. If cleared, I'd still start Wilson as a low-end WR2, even though the Chiefs have allowed the second-fewest fantasy points per game to wide receivers. If Wilson cannot play, Adonai Mitchell is the first receiver I'd look to add. Mitchell caught seven of nine targets for 142 yards and a touchdown last week, finishing with 27.2 fantasy points. I'd view Mitchell as an upside WR4. Omar Cooper Jr . could also see more targets, but I'd leave him on the bench. He had consecutive 100-yard games before going without a target against the Raiders. Mason Taylor is worth considering if you need help at tight end. He's drawn at least five targets in three consecutive games and faces a Chiefs defense that has allowed the fifth-most fantasy points per game to the position. Have a plan in the event of a Jonathan Taylor trade Jonathan Taylor has been mentioned in trade rumors, and there's a chance the Indianapolis Colts move him before Sunday. Managers who roster Taylor should add Seth McGowan (rostered in 21.6% of ESPN leagues) as insurance. If Taylor is traded (or made a Sunday inactive while the team continues to work on a deal), McGowan could see a much larger workload against a Cowboys defense that's allowed the sixth-most fantasy points per game to running backs. Nov. 4: Bhayshul Tuten questionable for Thursday night By Michael DiRocco, NFL Nation Jaguars reporter Jaguars RB Bhayshul Tuten (ankle, foot) likely will be a game-time decision for Thursday night's game in Baltimore, coach Liam Coen said Wednesday. Tuten, who is coming off the best game of his career (104 yards, a TD and a TD catch), did not practice at all this week. Tuten leads the Jaguars in rushing (492 yards). If he's unable to play, the Jaguars will likely start Chris Rodriguez Jr and behind him will be Ameer Abdullah and LeQuint Allen Jr . Nov. 4: Shedeur Sanders could start with Deshaun Watson in concussion protocol By Eric Moody Watson entered the concussion protocol Tuesday after reporting symptoms from a helmet-to-helmet hit in Sunday's loss to Pittsburgh. If he can't clear it in time for Week 9 against the New Orleans Saints , Sanders would likely start. He averaged just 11.9 fantasy points per game in seven starts last season, throwing seven touchdowns and nine interceptions. That's not encouraging for a Browns passing offense that ranks 22nd in passing yards per game this season. Quinshon Judkins led the Browns' backfield with 13 touches against Pittsburgh but scored only 6.9 fantasy points, as Dylan Sampson led the backfield in snaps and routes, finishing with 12.9 fantasy points. The matchup against the Saints is much better for Judkins, as they allowed the most fantasy points per game to running backs. I'd still view him as a low end RB2, but his workload could be reduced if Cleveland falls behind again. Sampson is worth holding in most leagues and has flex appeal in deeper formats. KC Concepcion was among Cleveland's leaders in routes against Pittsburgh but saw only four targets and scored 6.7 fantasy points. Harold Fannin Jr . (12 targets) and Denzel Boston (11) both saw more targets last week. I'd have a hard time trusting Concepcion or Boston in most fantasy lineups if Sanders starts.. Fannin is the one Cleveland pass catcher I'd feel comfortable starting. He caught 10 passes for 154 yards last week and has drawn at least nine targets in consecutive games. He's best viewed as a low-end TE1 if Sanders starts. Nov. 4: Sam Darnold out, Jacoby Brissett traded By Adam Rank Sam Darnold has been ruled out for Sunday's game because of a quad injury he sustained on Monday night. Darnold has not been placed on IR, so you're going to need to either release him (which is fine in single-quarterback leagues) or put him on the bench in superflex leagues. The latter makes the most sense for Drew Lock as a replacement option. However, if you're in traditional leagues with a realistic waiver wire, my first option would be to look at Malik Willis , who has averaged more than 20 points in Week 7 and 8. Willis has an advantageous matchup against the Detroit Lions , who have been very generous to opposing quarterbacks -- particularly mobile quarterbacks. The Lions are dead last against the pass (24.7 fantasy points per game). In additional quarterback news, ESPN is reporting that QB Jacoby Brissett is expected to be traded to the Baltimore Ravens over the weekend. While Brissett is a streamable option moving forward, this is great news for Ravens WR Zay Flowers . There is concern about Flowers this Thursday against the Jacksonville Jaguars , but he will move back to his low-end WR1/WR2 status if the deal for Brissett is completed. Nov. 2: How the move to Minshew impacts the Cardinals By Michael F. Florio The Arizona Cardinals named Gardner Minshew II as their starting quarterback on Monday for the Week 9 game in Seattle against the Seahawks. Minshew took over for Jacoby Brissett on Sunday against Dallas and threw for 188 yards and three touchdowns in a comeback win against the Cowboys, but faces a much tougher task next week against Seattle. The biggest question is what this means for the Cardinals' pass catchers, but here's how I expect the switch at QB to impact all positions on offense. While a QB change always adds some volatility, Trey McBride remains a must-start tight end. McBride is a must start regardless of who the QB is, but Minshew's tendency to target tight ends at a high rate keeps him as an elite option. In both 2023 and 2024, Minshew threw to tight ends 12% of the time, which is actually a higher rate than Brissett this season (10%). Due to volume, Michael Wilson will remain in play in most matchups. However, next week against Seattle is a week to get him out of your starting lineup. The Cardinals next three weeks are tough matchups for Wilson. Outside of Wilson, fantasy managers should avoid the rest of the Cardinals receivers for the time being. The running backs could see a bump in targets as well, as Minshew has always targeted running backs at a high rate. His career average is 17%, while Brissett is at 13% this season. As for Minshew himself, fantasy managers should stay away with the next three weeks being the Seahawks, Rams and Chiefs. For Week 9, some more favorable streaming options are Kyler Murray (or potentially Carson Wentz ), Malik Willis , Sam Darnold and C.J. Stroud .
