PckTme - Passing pocket time  Definition The number in seconds from the snap of the ball to when the QB releases the ball or leaves the pocket. Why does this stat matter for fantasy? Helps show a quarterback's play-style, as rushing/improvisational quarterbacks typically hold the ball longer. Running backs who consistently get tackled behind the line are less likely to gain yards and this might signal a bad offensive line.
